# Local support v2: first training-only seed

These are authored synthetic records, not captured project memories or fresh
acceptance data. The seed contains 32 cohorts and 68 target decisions across
eight declared scenario/template families. Every family has three queries and
one reversed-order copy of its first query. Order copies share ancestry and
do not add independent facts or families. Candidate and source IDs are opaque;
gold labels, critical flags, rationales and lineage stay outside model inputs.

Status: independent review covered all 32 cases and 68 target labels, including
the corrections in `review.json`. All annotation records are reviewed. This
records label review only; it does not establish generalization or authorize
training. Forty-three existing frame, label-binding and critical-registry tests
passed. The structural auditor still reports semantic verification and training
readiness as false because it cannot certify either mechanically.

The cases cover continuous elapsed time with resets, identity deduplication,
withdrawn proposals, fabricated quotations, complete-claim evidence ownership,
exclusive choices, delegated requests and missing observations. A primary
reason describes one sufficient cause for rejection; a case can have other
rejection grounds. A target with an unsupported material clause remains
unsupported even when another clause matches the query.

The critical registry binds three unique action constraints to complete fact
content and owned sources. Critical flags remain set for irrelevant queries.
There are nine supported critical opportunities, including order copies;
these are not nine independent critical facts or a retention measurement.

The structural audit reports 23 supported and 45 unsupported target decisions.
The exact tokenizer/frame check reports 292-397 tokens and at most 1,145
payload bytes, with every owned source retained. It loads only the already
frozen local tokenizer, with no model forward or hosted call. The mechanical
ancestry check against the old 127 cases records two inherited constructions:
Orin's owned-source conjunction and Vireo's exclusive choice. All eight rows
from those families are marked known-material derivatives. Historical reserved
partitions are retired for this candidate; an analysis-only copy puts old cases
in one bucket to discover connections without changing original records or
scores. Eight scenario families are not eight established new independent
constructions. Absence of another mechanical connection cannot prove ancestry
independence.

This seed is insufficient for training. It has no tuning or development
partition, no injected-instruction or unresolved-conflict cases, no empty or four/five-target cohorts,
and no eight-source, full-bound or overflow cases. Attribution, temporal and
unknown-condition coverage is sparse. Much of the evidence explicitly states
what it cannot establish; naturalistic cases with subtler missing support
remain needed. Do not infer generalization from this seed's declared family
count, its short frames or the integrity checks.

`inputs.json` is the inference data. `labels.json` binds each annotation to its
request digest. `critical-registry.json` binds unique critical facts.
`structural-audit.json`, `critical-audit.json`, `frame-audit.json` and
`mechanical-overlap-audit.json` record their separate checks. The family
catalog records declared construction ancestry; it is subject to review.

Before freezing, author self-review corrected Aster's owned evidence: the rule
target now owns the actual trace as well as the instruction. The initial
pending draft remains in a private archive. No frozen historical evaluation
was changed. No training, support score, quality acceptance, live installation
change or pilot day follows from preparing these records.
