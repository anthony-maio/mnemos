# Fresh corpus draft v1: preserved preparation failure

The draft passes v5 structural loading: 200 heldout sessions, 20 development
sessions, 200 critical facts, 200 answerable/no-answer queries each and 80 state
pairs. It is not ready for evaluation. No evaluated-model calls were made.

All 200 no-answer queries target a foreign profile, leaving no ordinary in-scope
unanswerable questions. Of 63 correction-tagged sessions, 43 have no correction
operation. Of 56 forgotten-replay-tagged sessions, 54 have no forget operation.
The author's log also overcounts noncritical facts: actual heldout totals are
200 critical supported facts plus 20 superseded and 20 forgotten facts, 240 total.

The raw draft and authoring log are preserved without correcting their claims.
The author reported reading only the schema and voice skill, not existing
corpora or tested prompts/outputs. That report and unique IDs do not prove
semantic independence. Long-session, hypothetical, noise, conflict, source-role
and replay coverage still need a meaning-based review. A separate revision is
requested; this draft is neither frozen acceptance data nor a model result.
